Anh Dương

Giải Pickleball CLB AES Mông Dương Tháng 9/2025 đã khép lại, nhưng tinh thần thể thao, sự gắn kết và niềm vui lan tỏa chắc chắn sẽ còn được duy trì và phát triển trong thời gian tới.

AES Mong Duong Pickleball Club Tournament – September 2025 — As part of the Wellbeing Month 2025, the AES Mong Duong Pickleball Club successfully organized the September 2025 Internal Pickleball Tournament, with enthusiastic participation from many athletes and lively support from the audience. This annual event aims to promote health, reduce stress, and create a meaningful playground that strengthens the bond among Company members.

Vibrant Atmosphere – Exciting Matches

The tournament took place on September 16–17, 2025, at the Housing Pickleball courts and Cau Den courts, attracting more than 50 athletes competing in two categories:

  • Men’s Doubles 4.5 Level
  • Men’s Doubles 3.5 Prospective Level

With a group-stage and knockout format, the tournament featured thrilling and competitive matches, showcasing true “Fair Play” spirit.

and Phung Duc Bac – Phung Duc Anh Hai

A Memorable Event of Connection

Beyond being a sports competition, the tournament was an opportunity for club members to exchange experiences, improve techniques and strategies, and raise overall playing standards. The enthusiastic cheers from the audience further fueled the excitement, turning every match into an unforgettable moment.

After the awarding ceremony, the Organizing Committee hosted a closing celebration party, creating a cheerful and bonding atmosphere that wrapped up the tournament with lasting memories.
